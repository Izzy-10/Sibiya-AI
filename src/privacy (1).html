Privacy Policy

Sibiya AI is a personal assistant built for the Sibiya family and ventures. This page explains what information is handled when you use it, and how.

What we collect

  • Messages you send — text you type is sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate a response. Messages are not stored on any Sibiya AI server after the conversation ends; they exist only in your browser session.
  • PDF uploads — files you attach are sent directly to the Claude API for reading and are not saved or logged separately.
  • Voice output — replies are read aloud using your device's built-in text-to-speech. No audio is recorded, stored, or transmitted anywhere.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your data with third parties.
  • We don't use your conversations for advertising.
  • We don't require an account, login, or personal details to use Sibiya AI.

Third-party processing

Messages and documents are processed by Anthropic's Claude API to generate responses. Anthropic's own privacy practices apply to that processing — see anthropic.com/legal/privacy.

Data retention

Conversation history lives only in your browser's memory for the current session. Closing or refreshing the page clears it. No conversation database is maintained by Sibiya AI.

Contact

Sibiya AI is built and maintained by Sizwe Sicelimilo Sibiya. Questions about this policy can be directed through the DevMzansi community channels.
