Get started with Sibiya AI
  1. Open sibiya-ai.vercel.app.
  2. Type your name in the Your name box. Other people see it in group chats.
  3. Choose how to chat:
    • Start solo chat to talk to Sibiya AI on your own. You sign in with Google first.
    • Create group to start a chat with friends, or Join group if someone gave you a code.
  4. In a group, tap Invite to share the link. Your friends open it, type their name and tap Join.
  5. To bring the AI into a group conversation, type @sibiya in your message.

You can write in isiZulu or English. Sibiya AI replies in the language you use.

Connectors

Connectors let an AI work with other apps, such as a cloud drive or a calendar.

Sibiya AI does not have connectors yet.

What works today

  • Attach a PDF in solo chat with the paperclip and ask questions about it.
  • Share a group invite through any app (WhatsApp, SMS, email) using the Invite button.

If there is an app you would like Sibiya AI to work with, tell us. See Contact and reporting a problem.

Usage and limits
  • Cost: there is no payment or subscription in the app right now.
  • Budget: Sibiya AI runs on a limited budget. If it stops answering for a while, try again later.
  • Solo chat: needs Google sign-in. In a very long chat, the AI looks at the most recent messages only.
  • Groups: each message can be up to 1,000 characters, and names up to 24 characters. When you call @sibiya, the AI reads the last 20 messages. A group shows the latest 100 messages.
  • PDFs: PDF files only, in solo chat. Keep them under about 3 MB. Larger files may fail to send.
  • Reply length: replies are kept short and chat-sized. Ask a follow-up if you want more detail.
Troubleshooting

The Create group and Join group buttons say "Connecting..."

Check your internet connection and reload the page. If it keeps happening, try a private or incognito window.

Google sign-in does not open

Allow pop-ups for sibiya-ai.vercel.app in your browser, then tap Continue with Google again. If you see a message with a code in brackets, send it to us.

"Something broke on my end"

Wait a few seconds and send your message again. If it keeps failing, the usage budget may be used up for now. Try again later.

The AI does not answer in a group

It only replies when your message contains @sibiya. Type it exactly like that.

I cannot hear the voice

In solo chat, check that Voice is on and tap the speaker icon next to a reply. Phones only play audio after you tap. Check that your phone volume is up.

My PDF will not read

Only PDF files work, and they should be under about 3 MB. Scanned pages that are only photos may not be readable.

The page looks old or broken

Refresh it, or open the site in a private window. Phones sometimes keep an old copy.

My message did not send in a group

Check your connection and try again. Your text stays in the box if it fails.

Sibiya AI for Education

Sibiya AI can be a study partner. It can:

  • Explain a topic in simple words, in English or isiZulu.
  • Quiz you or help you plan revision.
  • Summarise notes you attach as a PDF in solo chat.
  • Explain code and help you find mistakes, which is useful for coding students.
  • Help you practise writing, then give feedback on your draft.

Good to know

  • It can be wrong. Check important facts with your textbook or teacher.
  • Use it to learn, not to hand in as your own work. Follow your school or university rules.
  • Sibiya AI is not linked to any school, and there are no classroom tools yet.

A study group works well: create a group for your class, invite classmates, and type @sibiya when you need an explanation.

Features and capabilities
  • isiZulu and English: write in either and the reply comes in the same language.
  • Solo chat: one-on-one with Sibiya AI after signing in with Google. It greets you by name.
  • Group chats: live messages between people, with an invite link. Call the AI with @sibiya.
  • PDF reading: attach a PDF in solo chat and ask about it.
  • Voice replies: tap the speaker icon to hear a reply read aloud.

What it cannot do yet

  • Browse the web or look up live news.
  • Open photos or other file types. PDFs only.
  • Remember earlier solo chats once you close the page.
  • Take actions in other apps.
Conversation management
  • Solo chat is not saved. Closing or refreshing the page clears it. Copy anything you want to keep.
  • Group chats are saved. Tap the back arrow to leave. Come back with the same code or invite link and the history is still there.
  • Start fresh: refresh a solo chat, or create a new group for a new topic.
  • Invites: anyone with the link or code can join, so share it only with people you trust.
  • Your name: it is saved in your browser. Change it any time on the first screen.
  • Sign out: go back to the first screen and tap Sign out.
  • Deleting a group: there is no delete button yet. Contact us and we will remove it.
  • Replay a reply: tap the speaker icon next to it.

More help topics

Groups and invites
  1. On the first screen, type your name and optionally a group name, then tap Create group.
  2. Tap Invite at the top of the group to share the link, or give friends the code shown under the group name.
  3. Friends who open the link type their name and tap Join group. If they only have the code, they type it in the Join with a code box.
  4. Anyone in the group can type @sibiya to ask the AI something. It answers the person who asked.

Anyone with the code can read the group and post in it. Do not share private information there.

Account and sign-in
  • Group chats need no account. Your device gets an anonymous ID automatically.
  • Solo chat uses Google sign-in, so Sibiya AI knows who is typing. It receives your name and email from Google and never sees your Google password.
  • To switch accounts, tap Sign out on the first screen, then sign in again.
Privacy and your data
  • Solo chat messages are sent to the AI to get a reply and are not saved by Sibiya AI.
  • Group messages are stored so everyone in the group can see them.
  • We do not sell your data or use your chats for advertising.

Read the full Privacy Policy.

Safety and community rules
  • Be respectful. No harassment, hate or threats.
  • Do not share passwords, ID numbers, bank details or other private information in a group.
  • Sibiya AI can make mistakes. Do not rely on it alone for medical, legal or money decisions.
  • If you or someone else is in danger, contact your local emergency services. In South Africa, dial 112 from a cell phone.
  • To report a group or a message, see Contact below. We can remove a group.
Tips for better answers
  • Be specific. "Explain photosynthesis for a Grade 10 test" works better than "explain photosynthesis".
  • Say the language you want, for example "answer in isiZulu".
  • Add context, such as who it is for, how long it should be, or what you already tried.
  • Ask follow-ups to go deeper, shorter or simpler.
  • With a PDF, ask a clear question, such as "summarise page 2 in five points".
  • In a group, put your request after @sibiya, for example "@sibiya settle this: which taxi route is faster?"
  • Check important facts yourself.
