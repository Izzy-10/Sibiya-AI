Privacy Policy

Sibiya AI is an AI assistant built by Sizwe Sicelimpilo Sibiya. You can chat with it alone, or create and join group chats with other people. This page explains what is stored, what is not, and who can see it.

Solo chat

  • Removal: you can delete your own messages with the Delete link under them, and they are removed for everyone in the group. To ask for a whole group to be removed, contact us using the details below.
  • Messages you type in a solo chat are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate a reply. They are not saved by Sibiya AI and disappear when you close or refresh the page.
  • PDFs you attach in a solo chat are sent to the Claude API so it can read them. They are not saved or logged separately.

Group chats

  • Group messages are stored. Everything sent in a group, including Sibiya AI's replies, is saved in a Google Firebase database so everyone in the group can see it.
  • Anyone with a group's code or invite link can read it and post in it. Only share the code with people you trust, and don't post private or sensitive information in a group.
  • What is saved with each message: the text, the display name you typed, the time, an ID for your device (an anonymous ID, or your Google account ID if you are signed in), and any emoji reactions added to it.
  • No account needed to join a group. We never ask for a phone number or password.
  • Removal: there is no in-app way to delete group messages yet. To ask for a group or its messages to be removed, contact us using the details below.

When the AI joins a group

Sibiya AI only replies when someone types @sibiya. When that happens, the group's most recent messages (up to 20) are sent to Anthropic's Claude API so the reply makes sense in context. It does not read or send anything when nobody mentions it.

On your device

  • The display name you type is saved in your browser so you don't have to retype it. Clearing your browser data removes it.
  • Replies in solo chat can be read aloud using your device's built-in text-to-speech. No audio is recorded, stored, or sent anywhere.

Who handles your data

  • Anthropic processes the messages and documents sent to Claude. See anthropic.com/legal/privacy.
  • Google provides sign-in, and Google Firebase stores group messages, sign-in records and the anonymous device ID. See policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Vercel hosts the app.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data.
  • We don't use your conversations for advertising.
