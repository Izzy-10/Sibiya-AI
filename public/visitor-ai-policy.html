Visitor AI Access Policy

Product policy. Access is enforced by the server, not by this page or the Terms and Conditions. The application behavior and server configuration must remain consistent with this policy.

Who can use public AI features

Sibiya AI's public-facing AI features are intended for website visitors and regular users. Solo AI chat requires Google sign-in. Group AI is available to eligible group participants when the group host has enabled it; group participation may use guest/anonymous sign-in.

Founder/admin exclusion

The founder/admin account identified by the verified email configured as OWNER_EMAIL is denied access to public AI requests by a server-side check. The check covers requests sent to the public chat API, including solo and group AI requests. Changing or hiding the interface does not replace this server enforcement.

Keep OWNER_EMAIL configured in the deployment's secure environment settings. Do not put secret values in client code or share them in chat. If the setting is empty or incorrect, the founder/admin exclusion may not identify the intended account; verify the deployment configuration and test it after deployment.

AI processing and data

Messages and supported attachments sent to an AI feature are processed to generate a response. In group chats, context from recent group messages may be sent when the AI is called. The Privacy Policy describes the product's data handling and service providers; review it against actual retention, logging, deletion, and provider settings before publication.

Limits and responsible use

AI access may be rate-limited or temporarily unavailable. Responses may be wrong, so verify consequential information. Do not submit passwords, payment details, identity numbers, or other sensitive information. See the Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy.

Reporting an access issue

If a regular visitor is incorrectly blocked, or the founder/admin account can still obtain a public AI response, report the issue at ssibiya026m@ai.wethinkcode.co.za. Check the verified Google account and deployment's OWNER_EMAIL setting without disclosing credentials.
