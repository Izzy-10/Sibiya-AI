About Sibiya AI

Sibiya AI began as a personal project and grew into a small chat space where people and an AI assistant can talk together. It is built to feel like home for South African users: it understands isiZulu, greets you warmly, and keeps a calm, grounded tone.

  • Solo chat. Sign in with Google and talk to Sibiya AI one-on-one. It greets you by name.
  • Group chats. Create a group, share the invite link or code, and chat with friends in real time. Type @sibiya to bring the AI into the conversation.
  • isiZulu and English. Write in isiZulu and it replies in isiZulu. Write in English and it replies in English, with the odd sawubona.
  • Reads PDFs. Attach a PDF in solo chat and ask questions about it.
  • Talks back. Tap the speaker icon to hear a reply read aloud.

The name and the look

Umoya wesizwe sakwaZulu is the tagline. Roughly, it means the spirit of the people of KwaZulu. The logo is a Zulu shield (ihawu) with a beaded spine and crossed spears, with a gold S for Sibiya. The black, gold and red palette and the geometric borders draw on Zulu tradition.

How it is built

  • React and Vite for the app, hosted on Vercel
  • Firebase for Google sign-in and group chat
  • Claude, made by Anthropic, as the AI behind the replies

Group messages are stored so everyone in the group can see them. Read the Privacy Policy for details.
